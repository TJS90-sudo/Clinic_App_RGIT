Book Appointments via SMS

Simple, fast appointment booking using your mobile phone - no smartphone required!

SMS "BOOK" to 32123

How SMS Booking Works

Booking an appointment is as simple as sending a text message. Follow these easy steps:

1Send SMS

Text "BOOK" to our shortcode 32123 from your mobile phone

You: BOOK
To: 32123

2Receive Reply

You'll get an immediate reply asking for your preferred clinic and date

ClinicBook: Welcome! Reply with:
1 - Clinic name or area
2 - Preferred date (DD/MM)
Example: "Cape Town CBD, 15/12"

3Provide Details

Reply with your clinic preference and desired appointment date

You: Cape Town CBD, 15/12
To: 32123

4Confirm Booking

We'll send you available times and a booking reference number

ClinicBook: Available times:
A) 09:00 AM
B) 11:30 AM
C) 2:00 PM
Reply with letter choice (A, B, or C)

SMS Commands Reference

Here are all the commands you can use:

Command Purpose Example
BOOK Start new appointment booking BOOK
CHECK Check existing appointment CHECK REF123
CANCEL Cancel appointment CANCEL REF123
RESCHEDULE Reschedule appointment RESCHEDULE REF123
HELP Get help and instructions HELP
STOP Stop receiving SMS notifications STOP

SMS Costs

Standard SMS rates apply as per your mobile network provider. Most bookings require 2-4 SMS messages total.

ClinicBook SMS service is FREE - you only pay your network's SMS charges!

Tips for Successful SMS Booking

  • Keep it simple: Use clear, short messages with the exact format requested
  • Save the number: Add 32123 to your contacts as "ClinicBook" for easy access
  • Wait for replies: Allow 2-3 minutes between messages for system processing
  • Keep reference numbers: Save your booking reference (REF123) for future use
  • Check network coverage: Ensure you have good signal when booking to avoid delays

Emergency Notice

SMS booking is for non-urgent appointments only. For medical emergencies, call 10177 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately.
